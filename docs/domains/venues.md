# Venues

A Venue is a distinct business account type, not an ordinary Vendor category. It can maintain a venue profile, but it cannot offer the wedding-planning service or act as the assigned wedding planner.

When a Couple assigns a Venue, the Venue can view the limited wedding brief currently exposed by the server. This is read-only: the Couple and its assigned Vendor planner manage the wedding workspace.

Multi-staff venue operations, spaces, calendars, bookings, and organisation-level analytics are not currently persisted capabilities. See [organisations](organisations.md) and [ADR-004](../decisions/ADR-004-venue-organisation-model.md).
