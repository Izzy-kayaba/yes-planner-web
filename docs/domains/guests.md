# Guests

Guests receive an invitation link and submit an RSVP through the public invitation experience. The invitation workflow can collect attendance, party details, meal choice, and related guest responses.

Guests are not workspace users by default. Invitation tokens must be treated as private links and validated by the server. A guest response must be scoped to the invitation and wedding represented by that token.

Do not expose internal wedding records or other guests' private contact information on the public RSVP route.
