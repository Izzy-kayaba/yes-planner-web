# Couples

The Couple account owns the wedding profile and is the primary decision-maker for its wedding. Couple onboarding collects profile and wedding details before wedding-dependent experiences become useful.

The Couple can manage wedding workspace records, assign Vendors for services, assign a Venue for visibility of a limited brief, and revoke planner access. The Couple's ownership does not transfer when a planner or business is assigned.

Guest invitations and RSVP responses are scoped to the invitation workflow; they do not turn a Guest into a workspace collaborator.
