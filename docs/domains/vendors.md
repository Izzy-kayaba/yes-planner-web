# Vendors

A Vendor is a business account that can maintain a profile with a business name, contact details, services, service area, price range, and public portfolio. Services such as photography, catering, and wedding planning describe what the business offers; they are not account roles.

Couples can discover published business profiles and send a service request. Accepting a non-planning service does not give the Vendor general access to the wedding workspace. Wedding-planning access has a separate, explicit collaboration rule.

Claiming an existing listing is a review workflow. A pending claim cannot be withdrawn or duplicated; administrators approve or decline the claim.
