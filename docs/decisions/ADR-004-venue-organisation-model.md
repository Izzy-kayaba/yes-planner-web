# ADR-004: Venue organisation model

- Status: Accepted direction; organisation implementation deferred
- Date: 2026-10-08

## Decision

Venue is a top-level business experience and must not be reduced to a Vendor category. The current Venue assignment provides only a read-only wedding brief. Venues are not planners.

The long-term direction is a separate organisation and membership model that can support multiple staff and many weddings. That model is not yet implemented.

## Consequences

Do not grant Venue full workspace access or represent staff by sharing one Venue login. Design future roles as permissions/membership capabilities and preserve Couple ownership. Keep multi-wedding operations and SaaS entitlements marked as future work until server-side models exist.
